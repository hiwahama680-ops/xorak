# Xorak · خۆراک

A calorie, workout and fasting tracker made for Kurdish food. English and Sorani Kurdish.
It runs in Safari and installs to the iPhone home screen like a normal app. No App Store, no Mac.

## What's inside
- **4 languages:** Kurdish Sorani, Kurdish Kurmanji (Badini), Arabic, English. Right-to-left layout and Kurdish/Arabic numerals.
- **Easy setup:** a 6-step wizard builds your calorie, protein and water plan
- **Food diary** with 200+ foods (Kurdish & Iraqi dishes first), servings or grams, favorites, recent, saved meals, recipes, quick add, copy yesterday, undo
- **AI photo scan, "describe your meal" and AI coach** (Google Gemini free key or Claude key)
- **Camera barcode scanner** (Open Food Facts)
- **"What can I eat?" ideas** for the calories you have left, no AI needed
- **Train:** 11 guided home workouts (incl. gentle chair workout for seniors, 7-minute, Ramadan pre-iftar), gym log with sets/reps and personal records, 32 activities, steps
- **Fasting timer** (12:12 to 23:1) and **Ramadan mode** with suhoor/iftar countdown for 36 cities or your location
- **Progress:** weekly report, weight chart, BMI, body measurements & body-fat estimate, private progress photos, sleep & mood, levels/XP, streaks, 20 achievements
- **Daily score**, tip of the day, shareable daily card
- **Voice logging** in Kurdish, Arabic or English; the AI asks one quick question when a detail changes the calories a lot
- **Vitamins & minerals** (fiber, sugar, salt, potassium, calcium, iron, vitamin C) for every food and your day
- **Weekly meal planner** with Kurdish meals sized to your goal, shuffle and one-tap logging
- **Monthly report** you can save as PDF; small/big plate serving sizes
- **Shared AI** through a private server, so nobody needs their own key
- Large-text mode, dark mode, kg/lb, works offline, backup & restore

## Put it on GitHub Pages (free)
1. Upload all files in this folder to a GitHub repository (root of the repo).
2. In the repo: Settings → Pages → Source: "Deploy from a branch" → Branch: `main`, folder `/ (root)` → Save.
3. After a minute your app is at `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/`.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open that link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open Xorak from the home screen. It runs full screen and works offline.

## Turn on AI (optional)
- Gemini (has a free tier): get a key at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
- Claude: get a key at https://console.anthropic.com
- In the app: Me → AI features → paste the key → Save → Test.
The key is stored only on your phone and sent only to the provider you picked.

## Your data
Everything is stored on your phone. Use Me → Backup now and then.
Deleting the home screen app or clearing Safari data erases it.
